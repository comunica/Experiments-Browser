# Comunica master `483dc3c` against `e06d02e`: WatDiv and BSBM

A quick A/B of the 11 commits on Comunica master since the pinned `e06d02e` (2026-11-01), in all three browsers,
LDBC SNB left out. Comunica is `@comunica/query-sparql-rdfjs-lite` 5.4.1 at both commits, same store setup, same
harness and settings as the main run (1 warmup + 5 timed rounds, 3 repetitions, 60 s timeout).

## How it ran

Both page builds were made first, so that no build ran during a timed pass, and swapped in between passes. The three
passes ran back to back on 2026-11-04 on one machine (cloud VM, Intel Xeon @ 2.10 GHz, 4 vCPUs, 15.7 GiB RAM, Linux
6.18.44, Node 22.23.4; Chromium 153.0.8010.12, Firefox 155.0, WebKit 26.6):

| Pass | Directory | Comunica | Engines | UTC |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | [`2026-11-04-cloud-4vcpu-comunica-e06d02e`](2026-11-04-cloud-4vcpu-comunica-e06d02e) | `e06d02e` | Comunica, Oxigraph | 13:59 to 14:22 |
| 2 | [`2026-11-04-cloud-4vcpu-comunica-483dc3c`](2026-11-04-cloud-4vcpu-comunica-483dc3c) | `483dc3c` | Comunica, Oxigraph | 14:22 to 14:44 |
| 3 | [`2026-11-04-cloud-4vcpu-comunica-e06d02e-rerun`](2026-11-04-cloud-4vcpu-comunica-e06d02e-rerun) | `e06d02e` | Comunica | 14:44 to 14:51 |

Two controls say how large a difference has to be to mean anything: Comunica `e06d02e` in pass 1 against pass 3
(same build), and Oxigraph in pass 1 against pass 2 (Oxigraph did not change). Every raw file records the Comunica
commit embedded in its page. All 94,500 executions in the three passes returned the reference count.

## Result

- **WatDiv: no change beyond noise.** Comunica's totals move between −7.3 % and +8.3 %, while the same build moves up
  to +9.0 % between passes and unchanged Oxigraph moves between −7.3 % and +10.5 %. WatDiv's queries have no FILTER.
- **BSBM: Comunica is 2 to 12 % faster in total**, most clearly on bsbm-1k: −9.0 % in Firefox (new 951 ms to 1.00 s,
  old 971 ms to 1.15 s) and −11.7 % in WebKit (new 284 to 301 ms, old 301 to 336 ms, no overlap). In those two
  configurations the same build moved +2.6 % and −0.6 % between passes, and Oxigraph +3.0 % and −4.6 %.
- **Per template, the FILTER-heavy queries gain.** Q5, which compares variables in arithmetic FILTERs
  (`?simProperty1 < (?origProperty1 + 120) && …`), is faster in every browser and scale: 6 to 16 %. Q10, which compares
  variables with a number and an `xsd:dateTime`, is 12 to 18 % faster on bsbm-1k in all three browsers and mixed on
  bsbm-100 (−7.5 % to +0.3 %). Other templates are mixed. This is consistent with comunica/comunica#1947 (cheaper term
  and variable arguments in expression evaluation), but the commits were not measured one by one.
- **Not affected here:** the serializer commits (#1937, #1946, #1948) and the SPARQL endpoint commits (#1932, #1941,
  #1945): the harness consumes the bindings stream directly.
- **The comparison with Oxigraph does not change**: on BSBM, Oxigraph remains faster (bsbm-1k in Chromium: Comunica
  249 ms against Oxigraph 167 ms in pass 2), and on WatDiv the picture is as before.

A single slow page can shift a configuration by 15 % or more (Comunica `483dc3c`, watdiv-1 in Firefox: repetitions
of 662, 970 and 668 ms). The tables below therefore use the median over repetitions, per instance and for totals.
